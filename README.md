# MediMitra — Clinical Intake & Medical History Platform
> **"Your Health, Ready for Care"**  
> *AI-powered clinical intake and medical history platform that helps patients provide their health information before consultation and helps clinicians review structured patient information efficiently.*

---

## 🏥 About MediMitra
**MediMitra** (*Medical Companion*) streamlines outpatient department (OPD) clinical intake and case-history taking in hospitals by providing an accessible, multi-lingual touchscreen intake kiosk for patients and an integrated clinical review workstation for doctors.

### Key Capabilities:
1. **Patient Kiosk (Structured Intake Workflow)**:
   - 14-digit ABHA ID & QR Scanner or new walk-in registration.
   - 6 Indian languages (English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు, தமிழ், मराठी, বাংলা) with bilingual audio prompts.
   - Plain-language informed consent aligned with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) standards.
   - Adaptive clinical branch questioning (Chest pain, Fever, Headache, Abdominal pain, Cough, Other).
   - Real-time deterministic clinical triage (ESI Levels 1 to 5).
   - Medical document upload (Prescriptions, Lab reports, Discharge summaries, Investigations).
   - Document entity extraction & automated longitudinal medical timeline.
   - Printable official OPD token pass.

2. **Doctor Clinical Workstation**:
   - Real-time patient queue with triage urgency categorization (🔴 Red Flag, 🟡 Urgent, 🟢 Routine).
   - 10-section structured clinical summary (Chief complaints, HPI, Past medical, Past surgical, Medications, Allergies, Family history, Personal history, Review of systems, Previous investigations).
   - AI draft with mandatory doctor verification disclaimer (*"AI-generated draft — Clinician verification required"*).
   - Full inline editing, review, and confirmation (`History Verified ✓`).
   - e-Prescription builder with ICD-10 diagnostic coding and allergy contraindication alerts.
   - Standardized HL7 FHIR R4 Bundle export for ABDM ecosystem integration.
