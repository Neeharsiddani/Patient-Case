## 🏥 About MediMitra
**MediMitra** (*Medical Companion*) solves the critical OPD congestion and case-history taking bottleneck in Indian government hospitals by providing an accessible, bilingual touchscreen intake kiosk for patients and an integrated clinical decision-support workstation for doctors.

### Key Capabilities:
1. **Patient Kiosk (9-Step Intake)**:
   - 14-digit ABHA ID & QR Scanner or new walk-in registration.
   - 6 Indian languages (English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు, தமிழ், मराठी, বাংলা) with Web Speech audio prompts.
   - Plain-language informed consent with DPDP Act 2023 compliance.
   - Adaptive clinical branch questioning (Chest pain, Fever, Headache, Abdominal pain, Cough, Other).
   - Real-time clinical red-flag triage detection (ESI Levels 1 to 5).
   - Medical document upload (Prescriptions, Lab reports, Discharge summaries, Investigations).
   - AI OCR & automated longitudinal medical timeline (2022 to 2026).
   - Printable official OPD token pass.

2. **Doctor Clinical Workstation**:
   - Real-time patient queue with triage urgency badges (🔴 Red Flag, 🟡 Urgent, 🟢 Routine).
   - 10-section structured clinical summary (Chief complaints, HPI, Past medical, Past surgical, Medications, Allergies, Family history, Personal history, Review of systems, Previous labs).
   - AI draft with mandatory doctor verification disclaimer (*"AI-generated draft — Doctor verification required"*).
   - Complete inline editing, draft saving, and summary confirmation (`History Verified ✓`).
   - e-Prescription builder with ICD-10 differential diagnosis tags and allergy contraindication checks.
   - Official printable Indian Government / ABDM Outpatient Slip.
